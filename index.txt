# about

About me

I am a third-year Information Technology student at Dong A University, focusing on backend development. I have hands-on experience with technologies such as Node.js, Django, and Spring Boot, with a focus on building RESTful APIs and designing databases for web applications.

During my studies, I have worked on projects such as an e-commerce website and a training management system, where I was responsible for backend development and system logic. These experiences helped me understand how to structure backend systems and work with real-world data.

I am eager to learn, improve my technical skills, and contribute to real-world projects in a professional working environment.
